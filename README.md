# STUFF HIM IN THE FRIDGE

A vertical soft-body meme physics toy. There is a fridge. There is a man made of rubber. He has one facial expression. Stuff every inch of him inside.

- 100% single-file HTML5, zero dependencies, zero loaded files - every sound is synthesized live with Web Audio
- Real soft-body simulation: verlet blob with pressure conservation - pull his head, arms or legs; drag both feet at once to flatten him
- Full sequence: door slam, one tense second, violent fridge shake, giant spring launch with camera shake, zoom and particles
- 5 switchable scenes with original color worlds: KITCHEN POP, NEON ALLEY, VAPOR BEACH, TOXIC LAB, COSMIC VOID
- No scoring, no timers, no UI clutter - just see what shapes the guy can survive
- Phone multi-touch and desktop mouse support

## Play
- Online: https://sixboy666.github.io/fridge-stuff/
- Offline: download fridge-stuff.zip from Releases, unzip, open index.html

All characters and audio are original. No borrowed IP, no samples.
