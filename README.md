# PACK HIM IN

A level-based soft-body meme physics puzzle. He is made of rubber. He has one facial expression. Six containers. Fold him, stretch him, squash him - fit every inch inside.

- 100% single-file HTML5, zero dependencies, zero loaded files - every sound is synthesized live with Web Audio
- 6 unique containers: FRIDGE, SUITCASE, MAILBOX, GIFT BOX, TRASH CAN, COFFIN - each with distinct geometry
- Star rating system: 1-3 stars per level based on completion time, saved to localStorage
- Real soft-body simulation: verlet blob with pressure conservation, anisotropic squash, area conservation
- Eyes track drag point, face reacts to game state (calm, squint, dazed)
- 5 switchable scenes with original color worlds: KITCHEN POP, NEON ALLEY, VAPOR BEACH, TOXIC LAB, COSMIC VOID
- Phone multi-touch and desktop mouse support

## Play
- Online: https://sixboy666.github.io/fridge-stuff/
- Offline: download fridge-stuff.zip from Releases, unzip, open index.html

All characters and audio are original. No borrowed IP, no samples.
